Leadership scope

What I have been accountable for, at scale.

  • Global systems responsibility. Warehouse and transport systems used by 25,000 people across 370 sites in 51 countries at Kuehne+Nagel Contract Logistics.
  • Transformation. A multi-million-euro programme replacing the proprietary global warehouse platform, planned over ten years, with fortnightly stable releases along the way.
  • Budget and roadmap ownership. Platform roadmap governance and the annual development budget, negotiated and reviewed with regional, country and vertical stakeholders.
  • M&A and divestment. Routine systems integration as the business acquired companies; systems separation for the UK divestment to XPO, including data handover and continuity of service.
  • Regulated environments. Pharma, KRITIS, GDPR and defence-adjacent requirements across many countries, with signed-off validated specifications.
  • Board and executive leadership. First non-executive board director, then deputy CEO of an international logistics group through its transition to new leadership.
  • International teams. Direct and indirect teams across countries, including nearshore and offshore shared support centres.
