How working together starts

No long qualification process. A conversation, then a defined piece of work.

  • 1. A confidential first conversation. You describe the situation, I say honestly whether I can help.
  • 2. An agreed question and scope. One page, no ambiguity about what you are buying.
  • 3. A focused review or retained advice. Short and bounded, or ongoing where the situation justifies it.
  • 4. Written findings and next steps. Something the board or sponsor can act on, not a report that sits in a drawer.
