Where I am useful

Practical help in situations that are expensive to get wrong.

Delivery that relies on heroics

When releases slip, estimates drift and every project has five versions of the truth, the system is the problem. I help fix how work flows: ownership, cadence, quality gates and reporting that tells the truth early.

A transformation or integration losing coherence

Systems and processes no longer fit the business, or an integration is consuming money without a shared view of progress. I give the sponsor a short assessment, clear priorities and a practical recovery or integration plan.

AI adoption with real controls

Bounded use cases, pilots with defined success measures, and decisions about data, review, ownership and support. Useful applications earn their place; demonstrations do not.

How working together starts

A conversation, then a defined piece of work.

  • 1. A confidential first conversation. You describe the situation, I say honestly whether I can help.
  • 2. An agreed question and scope. One page, no ambiguity about what you are buying.
  • 3. A focused review or retained advice. Short and bounded, or ongoing where the situation justifies it.
  • 4. Written findings and next steps. Something the sponsor can act on.
